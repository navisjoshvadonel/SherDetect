| Region | Sample Format Characteristics | Empirical Fairness Accuracy |

| **LATAM** | A4 Standard, Spanish/Portuguese, BRL/MXN | `100.0%` |

## 4. 🔄 Human-in-the-Loop Override Feedback Loop

When a reviewer officer overrides an AI determination (`POST /api/documents/{id}/decision`), the system:
1. Records the ground-truth human decision into `ModelFeedbackTuner`.
2. Evaluates historical override patterns via `GET /api/governance/feedback-calibration`.
3. Recommends micro-calibrated scoring weights if false-positive or false-negative drift exceeds baseline bounds.
